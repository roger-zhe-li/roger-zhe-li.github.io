Roger Zhe Li

Senior Research Scientist · Huawei Ireland Research Center · Dublin

Dublin, Ireland

I am a Senior Research Scientist at Huawei Ireland Research Center in Dublin, where I also serve as a tech lead for recommendation and advertising research. My work sits where information retrieval meets large-scale industrial systems. Lately that means two things. One is semantic IDs: how to construct item identifiers that carry meaning, and how to consume them as features in retrieval-based ranking systems. The other is privacy-aware ad recommendation: keeping models accurate when personalized features are limited or must stay on the device.

Before Huawei, I spent several years as a Senior Data Scientist and Product Owner at adidas in Amsterdam. There I led computer-vision work on product representations and generative AI, and built recommendation and supply-chain solutions. I received my PhD in Computer Science from TU Delft, supervised by Julián Urbano and Alan Hanjalic. My thesis was on metric optimization and mainstream bias in recommender systems. Before that, I studied at Tianjin University (M.Sc., cum laude) and Zhejiang University (B.Eng.).

Research interests

Recommender systems

  • Semantic ID construction and item tokenization
  • Semantic ID features in retrieval and ranking
  • Cross-domain, multimodal and cold-start recommendation

Computational advertising

  • Ranking and CTR/CVR prediction under privacy and feature constraints
  • Cloud–device collaborative modeling

Evaluation and fairness

  • Evaluation methodology and metrics for recommender systems
  • Fairness and bias in recommendation
