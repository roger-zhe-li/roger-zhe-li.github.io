I am a Senior Research Scientist at Huawei Ireland Research Center in Dublin, where I also serve as a tech lead for recommendation and advertising research. My work sits where information retrieval meets large-scale industrial systems. Lately that means two things. One is **semantic IDs and generative recommendation**: giving items identifiers that carry meaning, so models can share knowledge across modalities, cold-start items and domains. The other is **privacy-aware ad recommendation**: keeping models accurate when personalized features are limited or must stay on the device. Much of this research is done together with colleagues at University College Dublin.

Before Huawei, I spent several years as a Senior Data Scientist and Product Owner at **adidas** in Amsterdam. There I led computer-vision work on product representations and generative AI, and built recommendation and supply-chain solutions. I received my PhD in Computer Science from **TU Delft**, supervised by [Julián Urbano](https://julian-urbano.info) and [Alan Hanjalic](https://www.tudelft.nl/ewi/over-de-faculteit/afdelingen/intelligent-systems/multimedia-computing/people/alan-hanjalic/). My thesis was on metric optimization and mainstream bias in recommender systems. Before that, I studied at Tianjin University (M.Sc., cum laude) and Zhejiang University (B.Eng.).

I am co-organizing the [IDEA workshop](https://idea2027.github.io/) on item identifiers at The Web Conference 2027, and presenting a [hands-on tutorial on semantic IDs](https://semantic-id-tutorial.github.io/) at CIKM 2026.

#### Research interests

**Recommender systems**

- Semantic IDs, item tokenization and generative recommendation
- Cross-domain, multimodal and cold-start recommendation

**Computational advertising**

- Ranking and CTR/CVR prediction under privacy and feature constraints
- Cloud–device collaborative modeling

**Evaluation and fairness**

- Metric optimization for ranking-based recommendation
- Mainstream bias and utility for non-mainstream users
